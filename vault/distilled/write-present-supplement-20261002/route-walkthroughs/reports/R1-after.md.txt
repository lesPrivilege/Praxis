2. 改后的文本：

````
## Sync fails with error 409

If the sync job stops with `409 Conflict`, the local index is ahead of the server. This happens only when two devices pushed within the same minute.

Do not delete the `.sync/` folder: it holds unsent changes.

To fix the error:

1. Reset the local index:

        syncctl index reset --keep-unsent

2. Run the push command:

        syncctl push

After the push, the error should normally be gone. If it still appears after two attempts, contact support and attach `~/.sync/logs/last.json`.
````
