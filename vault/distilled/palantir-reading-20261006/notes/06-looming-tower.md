节选标题「The Martyr」。

叙事范围：1948 年 11 月，Sayyid Qutb 乘邮轮从埃及亚历山大港赴纽约途中。出场主要是他一人（另提及埃及国王法鲁克、母亲法蒂玛、三位姐妹、父亲，以及转引的 Kinsey Report 与 Billy Graham）。地点是船上与回忆中的埃及。时间跨度从他的出身（上埃及土坯村）到 1948 年这次航行。

这一段讲的是**一个人怎样被激进化**：他受英国占领与法鲁克王朝的刺激，又因美国在战后支持犹太复国运动而感到背叛；节选末尾落在他对美国社会的道德判断（引 Kinsey Report，说美国人「little different from beasts」）与 Billy Graham 关于共产主义蔓延的警告。

## 节选要点

> "It was November 1948. The new world loomed over the horizon, victorious, rich, and free. Behind him was Egypt, in rags and tears."

> "The ideas that would give birth to what would be called Islamic fundamentalism were not yet completely formed in his mind; indeed, he would later say that he was not even a very religious man before he began this journey..."

> "Like many of his compatriots, he was radicalized by the British occupation and contemptuous of the jaded King Farouk's complicity."

> "What made this unimposing, midlevel government clerk particularly dangerous was his blunt and potent commentary."

> "...he worried about the advance of an all-engulfing Western civilization. Despite his erudition, he saw the West as a single cultural entity. The distinctions between capitalism and Marxism, Christianity and Judaism, fascism and democracy were insignificant by comparison with the single great divide in Qutb's mind: Islam and the East on the one side, and the Christian West on the other."

最后这一句是节选里最接近「认知框架」的陈述：他把诸多差异压缩成一条分界。
