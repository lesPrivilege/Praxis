# Abbreviations; Write for a global audience

来源：`TW-GOOGLE-ACRONYMS`、`TW-GOOGLE-TRANSLATION` · Google developer documentation team · 原语言：English。

## Source-language summary

The abbreviation guide expands unfamiliar terms on first mention, permits a short form in a heading with expansion in the following paragraph, and allows audience-dependent exceptions. The global-audience guide distinguishes translation from localization and internationalization. It asks authors to keep terminology stable, clarify referents and preserve helpful repetition. These are English documentation conventions; English punctuation and typography are not automatically Chinese rules.
