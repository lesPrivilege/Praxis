# Lesson Design; Example Data and Narrative

来源组：`TW-CARPENTRIES` · 作者/维护者：The Carpentries / curriculum contributors · 原语言：en

## Source-language summary / 原语言摘要

The curriculum starts with learner outcomes and assessments, then writes the content connecting them. A coherent example helps learners focus on the new idea while data selection balances authenticity, complexity and rights. Lesson maturity depends on actual teaching and feedback, including teaching by people outside the authorship group. These are educational design practices, not a claim that a language model can certify learning.

This review did not inspect the underlying learning-science studies or conduct learner trials. Curriculum claims about learning effects are not treated as measured effects in Praxis.
