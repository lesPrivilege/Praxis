## Source-language summary / 原语言摘要

The protocol gathers the audience, purpose and constraints, iterates sections, and asks a fresh reader model questions using only the document. Its valuable contribution is separation of author context from reader context. The final review still asks the author to check facts and impact. This is an authored interaction protocol, not a bundled executable validator. The repository explicitly qualifies the examples and separately describes some document-format skills as powering product capabilities.
