## Source-level finding

The official appeal route is to sign in at claude.ai with the suspended account and submit the linked appeal form; the form requires login. Anthropic names repeated Usage Policy violations, account creation from an unsupported location, and Terms violations as possible suspension reasons. Banned Free/Pro/Max users may still export available data or delete their account from the restricted screen; export availability may be limited. Organization-on-hold review is a separate path.

## Limits

It does not promise a review timeline, reversal, or full data export. Do not create substitute accounts or attempt to bypass a suspension; follow the official review path.
