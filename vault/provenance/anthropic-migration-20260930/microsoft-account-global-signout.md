# How to sign out of your Microsoft account everywhere

- URL: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/how-to-sign-out-of-your-microsoft-account-everywhere
- Retrieved: 2026-09-30
- Publisher: Microsoft Support (official first-party documentation)
- Evidence state: verified-online-no-page-snapshot
- Locator: “What happens after you sign out…” and steps in Advanced security options.

## Source-level finding

A personal Microsoft account can request Sign out everywhere in Advanced security options. Microsoft says it may take up to 24 hours to sign out from browsers, apps, and other sign-ins; Xbox is excluded.

## Limits

Consumer Microsoft account workflow. It does not by itself wipe a device, unlink it, revoke all application-specific sessions, or substitute for Entra admin actions in a work tenant.
