# Review devices signed in to your Apple Account

- URL: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649
- Retrieved: 2026-09-30
- Publisher: Apple Support (official first-party documentation)
- Evidence state: verified-online-no-page-snapshot
- Locator: “What devices on your Apple Account can do”, “When to remove a device”, removal steps, and “If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it”.

## Source-level finding

The Apple Account device list shows associated/trusted devices and allows remote removal/sign-out. Removed devices lose access to iCloud, Find My, other Apple services, and 2FA codes. A device still signed in may reappear, so Apple says to sign out on it or erase it for permanent removal.

## Limits

Account device removal manages Apple Account associations; it is not a substitute for organization MDM offboarding or clearing device storage.
