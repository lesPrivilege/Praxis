# What to do before you sell, give away or trade in your iPhone or iPad

- URL: https://support.apple.com/en-us/109511
- Retrieved: 2026-09-30
- Publisher: Apple Support (official first-party documentation)
- Evidence state: verified-online-no-page-snapshot
- Locator: Sections “If you still have your old iPhone or iPad” and “Remove your personal information”; transfer/backup, sign-out, erase, trusted-device removal.

## Source-level finding

Apple’s handover path is backup, transfer, sign out, erase all content/settings, and remove the old device from trusted devices. If the phone number changes, add trusted numbers before transfer to avoid loss of account access. Erase removes local data and disables Find My/Activation Lock while leaving iCloud content intact.

## Limits

Apple device-specific steps and menu labels depend on OS version. This is for transfer/disposal; migration to a retained device does not require erasing it.
