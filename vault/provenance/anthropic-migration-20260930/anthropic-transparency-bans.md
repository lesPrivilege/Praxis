## Source-level finding

Anthropic reports 11.4 million banned accounts, 398,000 appeals, and 42,000 appeal overturns for Jan–Jun 2026. Thus the historical Chat’s 11.4m figure is independently confirmed as an official aggregate. These counts are not a user-level probability, do not specify that all bans are permanent or unique people, and do not establish why any individual account was actioned.

## Limits

Aggregate enforcement data only; the page gives no denominator, detailed reason distribution, or per-account prediction.
