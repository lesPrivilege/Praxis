# What to do before you sell, give away, trade in, or recycle your Mac

- URL: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102773
- Retrieved: 2026-09-30
- Publisher: Apple Support (official first-party documentation)
- Evidence state: verified-online-no-page-snapshot
- Locator: “Use Erase All Content and Settings” for Apple silicon/T2 on macOS Monterey+; older Mac steps include deauthorize, sign out of iCloud and Messages, erase/reinstall macOS, stop at Setup Assistant.

## Source-level finding

Apple recommends a backup/file transfer, account/service sign-out, and factory reset before the Mac leaves the owner. Supported Macs should use Erase All Content and Settings; other Macs use the documented Recovery erase/reinstall process. Leave the device at setup assistant for the new owner.

## Limits

Applies when handing off or disposing of a Mac. For migration where the old Mac remains yours, it can remain in your Apple Account and need not be erased.
