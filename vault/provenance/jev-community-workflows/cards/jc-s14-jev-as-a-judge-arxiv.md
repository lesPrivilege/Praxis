# JEV-as-a-Judge: Accept When Confident, Escalate When Unsure

## 摘要

arXiv 摘要说明该研究比较 decision-only JEV 与 16 个 generative/reward-model judges，关注成本和 confidence reliability；摘要报告普通 preference 与 evidence-grounded factuality 接近强 judge、较难的 derivation/style 任务差距更大，并提出 confident accept / uncertain escalation cascade。
