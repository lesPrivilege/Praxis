# Whole-book review

The frozen book provides a credible path from recognizing a problem to selecting, calculating, checking and transferring a method. Its strongest teaching device is the small changed-premise counterexample: equal totals can conceal different obligations, a valid dependency order can violate resource capacity, and an external success can still lack business acceptance. Those explanations should survive final editing.

I recommend **targeted repairs before treating this revision as a finished teaching baseline**, with no wholesale rewrite. This review adds **four medium and eight low findings**. The strongest new executable defect is the enterprise acceptance check: the fixed ten-probe exercise closes on 1/1 or 9/9. All 37 supplied enterprise tests still pass. Both enterprise chains' original arithmetic matches independent calculations. No new high-severity defect was established within this review's scope; this is not a production-safety certification.

Input: public `lesPrivilege/Praxis`, `cd8d77b6166a6fc566f6aa09b4d9af3e11537408`, supplied on `cloud/saas-engineering-atlas-20261014`. Review branch: `cloud/saas-whole-book-review-20261014`. Only this review directory is changed. The running integrator's branch and all textbook/source content are untouched. Claude retains final writing, chapter organization and visual design authority.

## Reader outcome and editorial boundaries

| Intended ability | Judgment at the frozen revision |
|---|---|
| Recognize the problem | Strong: symptom tables and separate object/authority definitions make “completed but still owed” a concrete modeling question. |
| Select a method | Strong for the main routes: query shape, direction, units, resources and error tolerance determine selection. WB-04 and missing solution-family terms weaken a few research exits. |
| Calculate | The short examples are usable and generally preserve units, rounding points and denominators. WB-07 concerns prerequisite notation; WB-03/WB-09 concern a lost input condition and a misleading data label. |
| Verify | Counterexamples distinguish calculation, persistence, provider effects and UI evidence. Missing check routes and WB-01 obstruct parts of the advertised practical exercise. A passing structural validator cannot settle these teaching contracts. |
| Transfer | The map and exercises explicitly preserve a mechanism while changing domain conditions and authority. The full prose is necessary: a graph's reachability, a shared state word or an equal total cannot carry professional permission across industries. |

The [coverage report](coverage.md) records complete reads of all 33 cases, 12 foundations chapters and four enterprise chapters, plus navigation and indexes. No novice usability study was performed. Sequential prerequisites and chapter transitions are mostly coherent; the three learning routes provide meaningful alternatives to reading case numbers in order. Local reminders are useful at independent entry points and should not be removed just to reduce repetition.

The 565-heading inventory is available in [book-check-results.json](book-check-results.json). Headings were considered separately from explanations. Shorter display phrases may improve consistency, and the duplicated “演算：手算” in X02 is easy to trim. Neither sentence-shaped headings nor long paragraphs alone establish a defect. Preserve the inference, boundary condition and units in adjacent prose when shortening a label; Claude decides final wording and visual structure.

The book generally distinguishes standards, provider-specific practices and editorial synthesis. Selective new checks of [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#name-202-accepted), [Palantir permissions](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/action-types/permissions) and [Camunda 8.8 job workers](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/8.8/components/concepts/job-workers/) support the scoped distinctions used in enterprise chapter 3. The local five-stage completion model and ten-probe acceptance policy remain editorial/synthetic choices. [Source scope](sources.md) states what was and was not reopened.
